Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 31, 2026 · Eagle's Wings Network

We built Li3ht Clip to be simple, and our privacy policy is too. This page explains what we collect, why, and what we never do. Li3ht Clip ("we", "us") provides a video clipping and content creation service through our website and mobile application.

1. What we collect

  • Account email — only to create your account, log you in, and deliver referral bonuses. We never send spam.
  • Device identifier — a random anonymous ID on your device, used solely to prevent referral-code abuse and account farming.
  • Videos you process — uploaded files and downloaded source clips are used only to render your requested output. Raw uploads are deleted immediately after rendering; final outputs are deleted after 14 days.
  • Usage logs — basic technical logs (timestamps, error messages) to keep the service stable. No content is read or profiled.
  • Subscription status — if you subscribe to Pro, we store your account tier and credit balance. Payments are processed entirely by Apple / Google / RevenueCat; we never see or store your card details.

2. What we never do

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers or third parties. We do not train AI models on your content. We do not burn captions into your videos unless you explicitly ask.

3. Why we process your videos

Your video is processed only to deliver the service you requested: reframing, speed adjustment, thumbnail and metadata generation. Your content belongs to you.

4. Data retention

  • Account data: kept while your account is active; deleted on request.
  • Raw uploads: deleted immediately after rendering.
  • Final renders: automatically purged after 14 days — download your clips before then.

5. Your rights

You can request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account and all associated data at any time by emailing ispread2016@gmail.com. We respond within 14 days.

6. Children

Li3ht Clip is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above. Material changes will be announced in the app.
